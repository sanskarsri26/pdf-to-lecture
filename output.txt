**Slide Title**: Why Nondeterminism?

**Detailed Explanation**: Nondeterminism is significant in theoretical computer science for two main reasons: 1) **Power**:  Allowing for multiple parallel choices can lead to more efficient algorithms for certain problems.  It's a theoretical tool that can overcome limitations of deterministic approaches. 2) **Uncertainty**: Modeling uncertainty by considering all possibilities allows us to establish strong guarantees about system correctness, security, and performance, even in the presence of unknown or unpredictable inputs.  The question of whether true nondeterminism exists in the physical world is a complex philosophical one.  However, using a nondeterministic model is often useful for conceptualizing situations involving limited perspective or unpredictable factors.
